What Usually Goes Wrong in Orlando Homes

Walk through a house from the roof down and you pass most of the places a leak can begin. That is the route we take when inspecting a property in Orlando, and five spots along it account for most calls.

Attics: The top of the house takes on water when shingles are damaged or the seal around a vent pipe gives out. Insulation acts like a sponge, so it can hold a surprising amount before any of it shows on the ceiling. Once soaked, that material no longer does its job and holds moisture against the ceiling joists. We take out the ruined material, dry the framing and leave the roof repair itself to a roofer.

Bathrooms: A tub left filling, a sink with a blocked overflow hole or a toilet that runs over can soak a bathroom floor quickly. Tile looks waterproof, yet water gets through grout lines and around the edges of the room. When the bathroom is on an upper story, the water shows up as a wet patch overhead downstairs. Anything that came out of the toilet bowl should be treated as dirty water and cleaned with disinfectant.

Walls: Supply lines and drain pipes run inside the walls, and a slow leak there can go on for a long time. You might see trim pulling away, a discolored spot spreading or drywall that gives when pressed. Our Orlando technicians scan the area with infrared and probe it with a meter to mark the wet zone. Only that section is opened and dried, once a plumber has stopped the leak.

Tanks: The hot water tank sits out of sight in a garage, closet or utility space and stays full all the time. Worn parts announce themselves with moisture at the top connections, the drain spout or the pressure relief pipe. If the tank lets go, shut the cold inlet valve over the heater and turn off its power or gas supply. We pump out the spill and test the surrounding floor and wall bases, since water tends to run under them.

Drains: A clogged sewer line makes itself known at whichever drain sits lowest. Dark water rising in a floor drain or a ground-level shower means the main line is not carrying waste away. That water is Category 3, the most contaminated kind, and it takes protective equipment to clean up safely. Stop using water in the house and keep people and pets away until a trained crew arrives in Orlando.
