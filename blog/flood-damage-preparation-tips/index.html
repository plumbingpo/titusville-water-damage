How to Prepare for Flooding in Titusville, FL

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Titusville, FL

Nobody controls the weather, yet Titusville, FL property owners can still get ahead of flood damage with a few proactive steps. A prepared home takes far less of a hit when it floods, and the savings in restoration costs can reach the thousands. Below, our Titusville Restoration team lays out what Titusville homeowners can do to prepare most effectively.

Know Your Flood Risk

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. FEMA publishes flood maps that show whether a designated flood zone includes your property. Keep in mind that the majority of flood claims come from areas not designated as flood zones, so a lower-risk location still deserves attention. Look at how your land slopes, how it drains, and how close it sits to any waterway that might overflow in heavy rain.

Physical Steps to Protect Your Home

On the physical side, keep a sump pump with a backup battery installed and maintained, store valuables and key documents higher than ground level, grade the lot to fall away from the foundation, lift electrical panels and HVAC units above where floodwater could reach, seal any foundation cracks and add a waterproof coating, and clean gutters and downspouts while running the discharge out to 6 feet or farther from the foundation.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

Standard homeowner's insurance does not cover flood damage from rising water. Think about buying flood insurance from a private carrier or the NFIP well before a storm is forecast, because coverage typically starts only after a 30-day waiting period. Document your property and belongings with photos and video for insurance purposes. Store your policy paperwork where you can get to it, and learn your deductible and coverage limits.

Plan Your Emergency Response

Planning has to happen before floodwater is on the way. Keep a packed go-bag ready, and be sure of your evacuation routes. The contact information for your insurance agent should be within quick reach as well. If flooding is forecast, move valuables to upper levels, turn off utilities if instructed, and document your property's condition before the event.
