Where Water Damage Starts in Tampa Properties

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. We have ordered these five common Tampa calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Depending on the season, the break may follow a freeze, and it often shows only when the ice inside melts. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. In Tampa kitchens we often dry cabinets in place by sending air through small holes in the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Left alone, the damp air rises into the rooms above and the wood framing begins to decay. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Do not scrub or spray a large patch yourself, because disturbing it spreads spores through the house. We check for hidden dampness, set up containment and clear out the affected material for homeowners in Tampa.

How Drying Works on a Tampa Water Damage Job

A wet building left alone does dry eventually, but far too slowly. Moisture inside walls, studs and subfloors can linger for weeks, and that is plenty of time for mold to grow and wood to distort. Our job on every Tampa property is to speed that up in a controlled way.

Extraction is always step one, since pumping and vacuuming remove water much faster than any drying machine. Next, air movers placed around the room drive air over damp materials so the water in them evaporates. That vapor is collected by dehumidifiers and pumped away, keeping the air dry so the process does not stall. Without the dehumidifiers, the air movers would just move damp air around.

A technician measures the wet materials daily against readings from an unaffected area of the property. For most Tampa homes the target is met in 3 to 5 days, though hardwood, plaster and other dense materials need more time. The equipment comes out when the numbers say so, not when the calendar does.
