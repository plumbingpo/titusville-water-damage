Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Titusville Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Titusville and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Titusville

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
