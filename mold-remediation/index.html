Treat mold as a warning as well as a health hazard, because the hidden moisture feeding it puts your property's structural integrity at risk. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. At Titusville Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Titusville homes and commercial properties.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. Professional remediation means identifying the moisture source feeding the mold, setting up containment to prevent cross-contamination, removing affected materials safely with HEPA-filtered equipment, treating the remaining surfaces with antimicrobial solutions and fixing the underlying moisture problem so it does not recur. The result of that thorough process is mold dealt with at its source, never simply masked.

Each project starts with a careful inspection, and in many cases we test the air to learn which mold spores are present and at what concentration. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. IICRC S520 standards guide the whole job, which is done inside negative-pressure containment with HEPA air scrubbers running. Afterward we verify the results, with clearance air testing if you request it, to confirm the property is safe to occupy.

Warning Signs for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces
  • Moisture beading up again and again on glass, walls or anything cold
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation
  • Mold can get going after just 24 to 48 hours of dampness, so have leaks fixed within 24 hours
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
