How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.
  • Completed-repair photos and the scope document belong in your home records.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Titusville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
