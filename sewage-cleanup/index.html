A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Titusville Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Titusville, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Titusville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
